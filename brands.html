Brand questions

The short answers.

Does this change my checkout?
No. Node adds a same-day option to your existing checkout for eligible addresses — your storefront, branding, and flow stay exactly as they are.
What does it cost me and my customer?
Your customer pays the same rate as your standard shipping — Node prices same-day at parity, so there's no premium. You set the rate, radius, and cutoff, and the unit is priced to keep your margin intact.
Which platforms do you support?
Shopify today, with a native app and carrier-calculated rates at checkout. Support for other carts follows — the model is cart-agnostic by design.
Where can I offer it?
We're live in New York City and Northern New Jersey and expanding by measured demand. You can install now and turn same-day on the moment your customers' area is covered — checkout is never affected for addresses we can't serve.
How long does it take to launch?
About two weeks from install to your first same-day order — most of which is us placing your inventory. There's no integration to build.
What about my existing 3PL?
Node works alongside it. Your 3PL keeps the bulk of your inventory; Node requests small "micro-replenishments" to neighborhood nodes for the local, same-day slice.
