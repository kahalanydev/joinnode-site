Why it works

Speed was never a courier problem. It's a placement problem.

Couriers are already cheap and everywhere — every test run we make comes in under $6 within five miles. What's slow is inventory sitting in one warehouse, three days away.

Node fixes the placement. Your product waits on a shelf two miles from the customer, so the last leg takes minutes instead of days — and because there's no warehouse and no premium carrier, you can offer it at the same price you already charge for standard shipping.

Questions

The short answers.

How fast is delivery?
Same day — and in dense zones, often around 90 minutes from checkout to the customer's door. It works because your inventory is already a couple of miles away when the order is placed.
What does it cost my customer?
The same as the standard shipping you already offer. Node prices same-day at parity, so there's no premium at checkout — you set the rate and the delivery radius yourself.
Which platforms do you support?
Shopify today, with a native app and carrier-calculated rates at checkout. Support for other carts follows — the model is cart-agnostic by design.
Where is Node available?
We're live in New York City and Northern New Jersey, expanding metro by metro in the order our demand data prioritizes. Brands outside the current area can install and join the waitlist — checkout is never affected for addresses we can't serve.
Do I need a warehouse or new software?
No. You install the app and send a single pallet. Node handles placement, restocking, packing, couriers, and tracking. Most brands are live in about two weeks.
Is my inventory safe at a node?
Custody is tracked end to end. Parcels are sealed and dual-scanned at every handoff, deliveries are geostamped, and the network runs its own audits — so there is exactly one accountable custodian at every step.
How do node locations get paid?
Per order, with a capped fee, paid on a regular schedule. Early nodes also receive a signing bonus and a monthly floor while their neighborhood's volume ramps up. See For nodes →
