Node questions

The short answers.

How much can I actually earn?
It depends on your neighborhood's volume, but you start with a one-time signing bonus and a guaranteed monthly floor (typically $600–1,500) while demand ramps. As order volume grows, per-order pay takes over — and you always keep whichever is higher.
How much work is it?
About two minutes per order — scan the item, bag it, hand it to the courier. Most orders are scheduled in advance, so it fits neatly into the downtime you already have.
How much space do I need?
A small, secure shelf — roughly the footprint you'd give a single shelving unit in a back room. We bring the shelf; you provide the spot.
What if I'm busy or closed?
You set your hours, and the system only schedules pickups when you're open and available. If a moment is busy, the network routes around you — no penalty.
How and when do I get paid?
On a regular weekly schedule, direct deposit. No invoicing and no chasing — the earnings tab shows exactly what you've made in real time.
Is any of it risky for my business?
No. There's no cost to join and no equipment to buy. Parcels are sealed and scanned at every handoff, Node carries responsibility for the goods, and you can stop anytime.
